About.

Cao Xinan is a teenage painter who works big and fast, mostly in paint on stretched canvas. The earliest piece here is dated 2017; the newest was finished in April 2026.

The subjects come from everyday life: classmates mid-laugh, a friend with a lollipop, a bowl of noodles next to a laptop, a red bus with a banana on it. Faces get stretched, teeth get exaggerated, and words like ART, MUSIC and REST IN PEACE are painted right into the picture.

Every painting is signed and dated by hand in the corner, day / month / year.
