( 02 ) About

About
the painter

A teenage painter who works big and fast on stretched canvas.

Cao Xinan paints the people around him: classmates mid-laugh, a friend with a lollipop, two people eating noodles at their laptops, a boy in sneakers next to a red bus.

Faces get stretched, teeth get exaggerated, and words like ART, MUSIC and REST IN PEACE are painted right into the picture. Every canvas is signed and dated by hand in the corner.

MediumPaint on stretched canvas
SubjectsFriends, music, street life, places
